## 1. Quant Roles

* **Roles:** Quant Researcher, Quant Developer, Signal Researcher
* **Compensation:**
  * **Base Salary (Senior):** CHF 180–280K
  * **Grad Entry:** ~CHF 130–160K
* **Scores & Metrics:**
  * **Salary Score:** 9/10

## 2. AI / Data Science Roles

### [1] Google Zurich
* **Location & Type:** Zurich · Hyperscaler — largest EU engineering site
* **Roles:** ML Engineer (L4–L6), Research Scientist, Data Scientist, AI Research
* **Compensation:**
  * **Base Salary:** CHF 160–240K
  * **Total Comp (incl. RSUs):** CHF 240–320K+ at L5+
* **Scores & Metrics:**
  * **Salary Score:** 10/10
  * **WLB Score:** 8/10
  * **Average Hours/Week:** 40–45
* **Trade-offs & Culture:**
  * **Pay:** Sets the salary floor for all of Zurich tech; RSUs are substantial.
  * **Trade-off:** Very high interview bar (4–6 rounds); internal project rotation can feel directionless; layoff rounds in 2023–24 have reduced some of the famed job security.
