# 🗺️ Project-Based Learning Roadmap Prompt Template

Use this prompt template to generate a customized, actionable roadmap that connects **Nomad Life, Side Hustles, Career Growth, and AI Systems**. Copy the text below and paste it into an LLM (like Claude or GPT-4o), filling in or modifying the bracketed inputs to match your specific profile.

---

```markdown
# Core Goal
You are a world-class career strategist, veteran digital nomad, and AI systems architect. Generate a highly customized, hyper-actionable, project-based learning roadmap that seamlessly integrates:
1. 🌍 **Nomad Lifestyle**: Global travel, visa hacking, timezone management, geographic arbitrage, and travel productivity systems.
2. 💼 **Side Hustle**: Productized consulting, freelancing, micro-SaaS, and passive income engines.
3. 📈 **Career Development**: Transitioning to/finding high-paying remote roles, expert positioning, and public portfolio building.
4. 🤖 **AI Systems & Automations**: Automating workflows with n8n/Python/Make, leveraging AI coding agents (like Cursor, OpenCode, Aider), and building custom LLM/RAG/Agentic solutions.

---

# User Profile Inputs
Customize the entire roadmap based on the following user profile. Provide deep, non-obvious strategies for this exact combination:

- **Current Role & Tech Stack**: [e.g., Frontend Developer (React/JS), beginner Python, no cloud experience]
- **Current Skill Level in AI/ML & Programming**: [e.g., Comfortable with JS, knows basic Python syntax, uses ChatGPT/Claude daily but has never built LLM apps]
- **Primary Side Hustle Interest**: [e.g., AI Automation Consulting for local businesses, Micro-SaaS tools, or AI-assisted content strategy]
- **Nomad/Location Independence Goal**: [e.g., Relocate to Southeast Asia in 6 months (Thailand DTV/Bali), then rotate to Europe (Portugal D8/Schengen 90/180)]
- **Target Financial Milestone**: [e.g., $8,000/month total ($5,000 remote job + $3,000 side hustle)]
- **Weekly Time Commitment**: [e.g., 15 hours/week for learning and building]
- **Timeline**: [e.g., 12 Months, split into quarterly phases]

---

# Required Roadmap Sections
The roadmap you generate must be structured exactly as follows:

## 1. Executive Strategy & Mindset
- **Value Proposition**: Define the exact intersection of the user's tech stack and AI system capabilities that companies or clients will pay premium rates for.
- **Geographic Arbitrage Loop**: Identify specific high-income earning markets (e.g., US/UK/EU remote clients) and match them with optimal low-cost nomad hubs (e.g., Chiang Mai, Tbilisi, Medellín) based on the user's passport/visa goals.

## 2. Core Skill Progression & Tech Stack
Provide a prioritized list of skills and tools to learn, ordered from foundational to advanced.
- **AI Orchestration & Backend**: (e.g., Python, FastAPI, n8n, CrewAI/LangGraph, Supabase, Qdrant/pgvector)
- **AI-Augmented Development**: Explain how to master AI coding agents (Cursor, OpenCode, Aider) to build 5x faster.
- **Nomad Infrastructure**: Tools for async remote work (Notion, Loom, Slack, Git, Docker, Coolify).

## 3. Quarterly Milestones (12-Month Timeline)
Divide the timeline into 4 distinct phases:
- **Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundations & Personal AI System**
  - Focus: Learning the core tech stack and building a personal system to optimize productivity and organize research.
- **Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Portfolio Development & First Client**
  - Focus: Building public proof-of-work (GitHub/LinkedIn) and landing the first small freelance or consulting gig.
- **Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Going Nomad & Productizing Offerings**
  - Focus: Transitioning to the first nomad base, managing timezone overlaps, and scaling a side hustle from hourly billing to productized services.
- **Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Systems Automation & Passive Income**
  - Focus: Deploying a micro-SaaS, setting up automated lead-generation or content pipelines, optimizing taxes, and stabilizing recurring income.

## 4. The 3 Core Projects (Project-Based Learning)
For each project, detail:
- **Project Concept & Objective**: A real-world application (no generic tutorials) that solves a business or nomad problem.
- **Intersection of Pillars**: Explicitly state how this project bridges Nomad, Career, Side Hustle, and AI Systems.
- **Step-by-Step Tech Stack**: The backend, database, vector DB, LLM APIs, and orchestration tools to use.
- **Side Hustle / Career Value**: How to showcase this project to remote employers or package it as a paid B2B consulting offer.
- **Nomad / Operational Utility**: How the user can run this specific AI system to manage their own visa runs, flight tracking, local housing search, or client lead generation.

## 5. Nomad Operations, Visas & Taxes
- **Visa Blueprint**: Detail 2-3 specific visa strategies (e.g., Thailand DTV, Portugal D8, Spain Digital Nomad Visa, Georgia 365-day stay) that match the user's passport constraints.
- **Timezone Overlap Management**: Strategies for coordinating meetings and async communication with clients in different timezones.
- **Tax & Business Setup**: A high-level overview of structures (e.g., Wyoming LLC, Estonian e-Residency, UK Ltd) to handle payments globally and minimize tax liabilities.

## 6. Actionable Resources Directory
- **Remote Job Boards**: Curated sites matching the skill level (Himalayas, Working Nomads, Wellfound, Otta).
- **Freelance & Consulting Channels**: Platforms and tactics for inbound client acquisition (Contra, Toptal, LinkedIn Authority).
- **Nomad Financials**: Recommended global banking services (Wise, Revolut, Charles Schwab) and travel health insurance (SafetyWing).
```
