## 2. Part-Time Work Rights

| Country | Hours Allowed | Restrictions | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇨🇭 **Switzerland** | 15 hrs/week during term | Varies by canton; permit required | Work permit usually tied to residence permit |
| 🇩🇪 **Germany** | 120 full days or 240 half days/year | Must not endanger studies | Students from some countries need prior approval |
| 🇬🇧 **UK** | 20 hrs/week during term | Full-time during holidays | Employer must verify visa; Tier 4/Student visa |
| 🇮🇪 **Ireland** | 20 hrs/week during term | 40 hrs/week during holidays | One of the most flexible regimes |
| 🇨🇦 **Canada** | Unlimited on-campus; 20 hrs off-campus | Off-campus eligibility after 6 months (some exceptions post-2024 policy changes) | Co-op/internship permits available separately |

## 3. Post-Study Work Options (PSW)

| Country | Duration | Eligibility | Path to PR? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇨🇭 **Switzerland** | 6 months job-search permit | After Master's/PhD from Swiss university | Difficult; requires employer sponsorship, then long-term permit |
| 🇩🇪 **Germany** | 18 months job-seeker visa | After any degree from German university | Yes — via EU Blue Card or skilled worker residence permit |
| 🇬🇧 **UK** | 2 years (Graduate Visa); 3 yrs for PhD | After degree from a licensed sponsor institution | Possible via Skilled Worker visa → ILR (5 yrs) |
| 🇮🇪 **Ireland** | 1–2 years (Third Level Graduate Programme) | 1 yr for NFQ Level 8; 2 yrs for Level 9/10 | Yes — via Critical Skills Employment Permit or General Employment Permit |
| 🇨🇦 **Canada** | 1–3 years (PGWP) | Length tied to program duration | Yes — strong pathways via Express Entry, PNPs |

> 🏆 **Canada and Germany** offer the most accessible long-term immigration pathways. The UK is competitive but costlier. Switzerland is the most restrictive.

## 7. Visa Process & Complexity

| Country | Visa Type | Processing Time | Complexity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🇨🇭 Switzerland | Student Visa (Type D) | 6–12 weeks | Moderate; requires cantonal approval |
| 🇩🇪 Germany | National Visa (Student) | 8–12 weeks | Moderate; blocked account (€11,208/yr) required |
| 🇬🇧 UK | Student Visa (Tier 4) | 3–8 weeks | Moderate; CAS required from university |
| 🇮🇪 Ireland | Study Visa (C or D) | 4–8 weeks | Relatively straightforward |
| 🇨🇦 Canada | Study Permit | 4–16 weeks (varies widely) | Moderate; SDS stream speeds up process for some |

> ⚠️ **Germany** requires a blocked bank account (Sperrkonto) showing ~€11,208 before visa issuance.

## 10. Overall Summary & Best Fit

| Profile | Best Destination |
|---|---|
| 💰 Tightest budget | **Germany** — lowest cost of living; minimal visa hurdles |
| 🏆 Best immigration pathway | **Canada** — PGWP + Express Entry is one of the world's clearest routes |
| 🗣️ No language learning desired | **UK or Ireland** — fully English-speaking; no adaptation needed |
| 🔬 Research / STEM excellence | **Switzerland** — ETH Zurich, EPFL rank among the world's top 10 |
| ⚖️ Balance of all factors | **Germany** — affordable, safe, good work rights, strong PSW options |
| 🤝 Friendliest culture for newcomers | **Ireland** — welcoming, English-speaking, strong job market in tech/pharma |
