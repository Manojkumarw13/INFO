ETH Zurich + University of Zurich · Zurich
MSc in Quantitative Finance (MScQF)
Quantitative Finance English #1 Europe · #4 World
Overview
A joint program offered by the Department of Finance (UZH) and the Department of Mathematics (ETH Zurich). Ranked #1 in Europe and #4 worldwide by RiskNet Quant Finance Master's Guide 2026. Combines economic theory, financial markets, and rigorous mathematical methods (probability, statistics, numerical analysis). FinTech topics are embedded across several lectures. The program is run by world-class academics and leading financial practitioners, with strong industry ties to the Zurich financial hub (banks, insurance, consulting firms).
Quick Facts
  • Duration: ~1.5 years (2 semesters coursework + Master's thesis)
  • Language: 100% English
  • Degree Awarded: MSc ETH UZH (joint)
  • Credits: 90 ECTS
  • Location: Zurich (dual campus)
  • Start: Mid-September (Fall only)
Key AI / Quant Finance Courses
  • Probability Theory & Statistics for Finance
  • Stochastic Calculus & Numerical Methods
  • Asset Management & Risk Management
  • Financial Econometrics & Big Data
  • FinTech electives (multiple lectures)
  • Machine Learning applications in finance (via ETH electives)
Admission Requirements
  • Bachelor's from state-accredited university
  • Background in economics, finance, mathematics, physics, engineering or data science
  • English proficiency: C1 (IELTS ~7.0 / TOEFL ~100)
  • Programming: at least one language (Julia, Python, R, or Matlab)
  • Strong background in probability, statistics & linear algebra
  • Very competitive admission — no entitlement to admission

EPFL École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne · Lausanne
MSc in Financial Engineering (MFE)
Financial Engineering English QS #36 World
Overview
A multidisciplinary program offered by EPFL's College of Management of Technology in partnership with the Swiss Finance Institute. Faculty spans mathematics, statistics, computer science, operations research and communications. The curriculum integrates cutting-edge machine learning techniques with classical quantitative finance methods. The program concludes with a 6-month industry internship at leading firms like Pictet, Julius Bär, UBS, and Mercuria, providing hands-on experience and hiring connections. 92% full-time employment within 1 year of graduation.
Quick Facts
  • Duration: 2 years (120 ECTS — 3 semesters coursework + 6-month internship)
  • Language: 100% English
  • Degree: MSc EPFL
  • Location: Lausanne (between Geneva & Zurich)
  • Start: Mid-September
Key AI / Quant Finance Courses
  • Machine Learning in Finance
  • Stochastic Calculus
  • Derivatives Pricing
  • Quantitative Risk Management
  • Data Science & Statistical Inference
  • Optimization & Numerical Methods
  • Interest Rate Models
  • Electives: Deep Learning, Big Data
Admission Requirements

  • Strong foundation: analysis, linear algebra, probability, statistics
  • Programming: C/C++/Python/Java or Matlab/Mathematica
  • English proficiency required (spoken and written)
  • 3 letters of recommendation (from professors)
  • Motivation letter
  • Transcripts + GPA (competitive)
  • Swiss UAS (FHS/HES) degree holders are NOT eligible

Finance + FinTech English SFI Accredited

For International Applicants
Tips for Indian Students Applying to Swiss Programs
01
Apply Early — Especially for Visas
Switzerland's Type D National Visa takes 8–12 weeks to process. For ETH/UZH, the sole deadline is January 15 — missing it means waiting a full year. For EPFL, the December 15 deadline is strongly recommended for non-EU students. Start your application 3–4 months before deadlines.
02
Build a Strong Quantitative Profile
All five programs are math-heavy. IIT / NIT / BITS students with strong CGPA (8.0+ preferred) in engineering, mathematics, or CS have competitive profiles. Courses in probability, statistics, linear algebra, and at least one programming language are essential prerequisites.
03
GMAT/GRE — Only for HSG
The HSG MBF explicitly weights GMAT/GRE scores heavily (up to 60% of the selection score). ETH/UZH and EPFL do not require GMAT/GRE, but a strong score can still support your application. Focus on GRE Quant (160+) if applying to HSG.
04
English Proficiency Matters
A C1-level certificate is required for most programs. Accepted tests: IELTS (7.0+), TOEFL iBT (100+), Cambridge C1/C2. UZH requires the certificate before final matriculation, even if not during pre-selection. Don't submit an expired certificate (USI requires ≤3 years validity).
05
Plan Finances Carefully
Switzerland is expensive. Monthly living costs in Zurich/Lausanne: CHF 1,800–2,500 (₹1.67–2.3 L). St. Gallen and Lugano are 20–30% cheaper. Budget CHF 25,000–30,000/year total. Open a blocked bank account for your visa application showing proof of funds before arrival.
06
Chase Scholarships Actively
The EPFL Excellence Fellowship (CHF 50,000+/year) is transformative but highly competitive. Also explore: Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships (apply via Education India portal), Inlaks Shivdasani Foundation (India), DAAD (for Europe), and Tata / industry scholarships. Apply simultaneously.
07
Motivation Letter is Critical
Swiss universities don't require standardized essays but take motivation letters seriously. Articulate clearly: why this specific program (not just "Switzerland"), your research/finance interests, relevant projects or internships, and how the program fits your 5-year plan. Avoid generic templates.
08
Learn Basic German or Italian
While all 5 programs are taught in English, daily life in Zurich and St. Gallen is largely German-speaking. Lugano is Italian-speaking. Even basic A2-level proficiency opens doors to job fairs, local networking, and housing. USI even offers a free Italian course for enrolled students.
09
Understand Work Rights
On a Swiss student permit, you can work up to 15 hours/week during term (per Zurich's AWA regulations). This helps offset living costs but cannot fund full tuition. Don't rely on part-time work — it's supplementary. Many programs also have structured internship pathways (EPFL's 6-month MFE internship is part of the degree).
